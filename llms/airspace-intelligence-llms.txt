# Airspace Intelligence

> Air Space Intelligence (ASI) builds AI software for mission-critical, geospatial operations across aviation, defense, logistics, and energy. Its Prescience platform fuses data, models domains, runs predictive simulations, and optimizes complex operations in real time. ASI holds an $875M FAA contract to help modernize the U.S. National Airspace System.
